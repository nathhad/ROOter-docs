Routers Supported by ROOter

Any router that has at least 16 meg of flash memory and at least 128 meg of RAM, a USB port and is supported by OpenWrt can have a full featured ROOter firmware made for it. .Routers with 8meg of flash and at least 64meg of RAM are limited to having only part of the features of a full ROOter. They have the full modem support but are lacking many router level features. These routers are not recommended for use with ROOter.

Since there are a large number of routers supported by ROOter, a number of which are not official supported by OpenWrt, and more are being added all the time you should check the ROOter Autobuilds Firmware page to see if the router you are interested in is supported.
