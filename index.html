Welcome to Of Modems and Men

This is the home of the ROOter project, open source firmware for over one hundred different routers that provides extensive support for the use of USB Cellular modems in routers that don't support this from the factory. It is based on firmware from the OpenWrt project.

The goal of this project is to provide support for a large number of USB modems and have them operate at full speed on your Provider's Network. Plus it provides detailed information about your connection such as signal strength and cell tower details.
