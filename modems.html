Modems Supported by ROOter

One of the goals of ROOter was to support as many modems as possible and allow them to operate at their maximum speed. To this end we have constantly added new modems to the supported list. This started with support for many 3G modems and has since expanded to include most popular 4G and 5G models as well.

Support is available for modems that use the USB port, the PCie interface and the M.2 interface.

As well as modems, ROOter also has support for tethered Android and iPhones phones. You can connect your phone to your router using a USB cable and use it to provide Internet to your Network.

iPhone and Android Phones

Both the iPhone and the Android phones can be tethered to a router by the use of a USB cable. Turn on tethering in the phone and it will provide Internet to the router.
